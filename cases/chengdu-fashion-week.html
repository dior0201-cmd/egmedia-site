The project

Fashion as a city-wide experience.

The 2018 programme extended fashion beyond the runway into retail, public spaces and city life. It included a consumption-season pop-up carnival, designer exhibitions and the “Chinese Design in Chengdu” industry forum.

An integrated platform for fashion, retail and city audiences, combining live programming with brand and commercial activation.

Role & contribution

Project production and integrated operations, commercial development, sponsorship, resource integration, communications, and project coordination.
