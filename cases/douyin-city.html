DOUYIN CITY

2019–2021

A multi-year cultural platform connecting digital culture, urban spaces, creators and audiences through cultural experiences and entertainment IP.

Overview

Douyin City was a multi-year urban cultural programme initiated by Douyin, featuring different annual themes and city-based experiences.

EGMedia served as the Creative & Execution Partner, contributing to creative planning, experience design, visual communication, content integration, production and on-site delivery.
