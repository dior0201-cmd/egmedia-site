The project

Taking digital culture into the city.

The series brought a digital platform into physical city life. Representative editions included Douyin Tianfu in 2019, the Idol Festival in 2020 and Shenzhen’s Dream Space in 2021.

A coordinated programme across content, public space, creators and commercial partners, with a local delivery structure for each edition.

Role & contribution

Overall planning, local activation, government and venue coordination, creator and MCN organisation, brand partnerships, and on-site delivery.

Experience attribution

Founder and historical operating entities. Not presented as current Hong Kong company contracts or revenue.
