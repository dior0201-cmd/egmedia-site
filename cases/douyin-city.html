Platform IP · City experiences / 2019–2021

Douyin InCity

Taking digital culture into the city.

Project context

The series brought a digital platform into physical city life. Representative editions included Douyin Tianfu in 2019, the InCity Idol Gala in 2020 and Shenzhen’s Dream Space in 2021.

A coordinated programme across content, public space, creators and commercial partners, with a local delivery structure for each edition.

Role & contribution

Overall planning, local activation, government and venue coordination, creator and MCN organisation, brand partnerships, and on-site delivery.

Experience attribution

Selected experience of the founder and historical operating entities.
